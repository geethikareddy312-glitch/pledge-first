Customers pledge once

Goal reached: sent for production

Everyone who pledged is told the product is going into production. They pay nothing yet.

Made: backers pay

Once the product is manufactured, backers are told it is ready and pay the early-bird price to get it. They only pay for something that really exists.

Goal missed: it ends cheaply

The founder changes or drops the idea with no money spent on production, and backers are never charged.
